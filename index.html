About

I am an applied researcher and engineer with 6+ years of experience building real-world perception and decision-making systems for autonomous platforms. My work spans multi-sensor fusion, deep learning, and ROS 1/2 system integration — taking systems from algorithm design through live deployment on instrumented testbeds and vehicles.

I received my Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences and Technology from UCLouvain (Belgium), where I developed depth-vision terrain recognition for prostheses and exoskeletons, and my M.Sc. in Electronics Engineering from Flinders University (Australia).
