About

I build perception systems that run on real infrastructure and real vehicles. My work centers on LiDAR-based perception, multi-sensor fusion, and deep learning for intelligent transportation systems — from a deployed smart intersection in Greensboro, NC, to drive-by-wire integration of an autonomous Chrysler Pacifica.

I received my Ph.D. from Université catholique de Louvain (Belgium), where I worked on depth-vision terrain recognition for powered prostheses and exoskeletons, and my M.Sc. from Flinders University (Australia).
